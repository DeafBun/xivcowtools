Teamcraft Converter

Take a remakeplace .json file, and turn it into a teamcraft list! None of the other ones dealt with wallpapers/dyes, so I made my own!

Open Teamcraft

Redesigner

Take the current file of your house in game, and then upload your new house file you have yet to build!

This will output a "junkpile" json, which is your new house with all things in common with your own house placed where they need to be, and everything NOT in common with your old house placed in a junk pile at the origin, for easy cleaning!

This will also output a json of just the stuff you need to get for use with pyshopper, along with an associated teamcraft list generation link.
